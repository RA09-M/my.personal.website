Project Overview

Turning receivables data into useful information.

This sample portfolio project was created to demonstrate practical Accounts Receivable and Microsoft Excel skills.

The report tracks fictional customer invoices, payments, outstanding balances, overdue days, and aging categories. It also includes a dashboard designed to give management a quick view of the receivables position.

The project uses fictional information only and is intended as a demonstration of accounting and reporting skills.
